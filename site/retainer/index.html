These terms apply when you and ZASTRAS LTD, trading as Zastras Practical AI (“we”), agree an ongoing retainer for a workflow we have reviewed or built with you. ZASTRAS LTD is registered in Scotland under number SC832156, with its registered office at 48 West George Street, 2nd Floor, Glasgow, Scotland, G2 1BP.

They sit alongside a short order form that sets out the details for your firm. They are for businesses and professional practices, not for consumers. Our current prices are on the home page.

In short

  • A retainer is for a workflow that is in everyday use and needs ongoing monitoring and changes. If a handover would do, we will say so.
  • It costs between £400 and £900 per month, excluding VAT. The fee for your retainer is set in the order form.
  • There is no minimum term. Either of us can end it with 30 days’ written notice.
  • A person at your firm still reviews what matters. We will not take a review point out without your written agreement.
  • We tell you plainly when something goes wrong, including when it is our doing.

When a retainer makes sense

Most workflows do not need a retainer. Once a workflow has been tested and your team can run it, a handover and some training may be all that is needed.

A retainer makes sense when there is ongoing work that someone has to do, for example when:

  • the inputs change often, so the workflow needs checking against new kinds of examples;
  • exceptions and failures need regular review, and the workflow needs adjusting in response;
  • the tools or AI models the workflow depends on change, and it has to be tested again afterwards;
  • your rules, clients or services change, and the workflow has to change with them; or
  • your team is still building confidence, and adoption needs tracking and support.

If none of these applies, we will recommend a handover instead of a retainer. If a retainer later stops being justified, we will tell you and recommend reducing or ending it (see reviewing and ending).

Requirements for a retainer

We agree a retainer only when all of the following are in place.

  1. A workflow in use. It is a workflow we have reviewed and normally built or piloted with you, so we know how it works. We will consider a workflow built by someone else only after reviewing it ourselves.
  2. A reason for ongoing care. At least one of the situations above applies, and we have said which in writing.
  3. A named owner at your firm. One person with the authority to approve changes and receive our reports, and someone to cover when they are away.
  4. Human review in place. The review and escalation points set up during the pilot are used by named people. We do not run a retainer on a workflow whose outputs go out without a person checking what matters.
  5. A baseline and measures. We have agreed how we will tell whether the workflow is working, such as time per task, how often drafts need correcting, or response times. If these were not set during the review or pilot, we set them in the first month.
  6. Agreed information and tools. You have told us which information and which tools the workflow may use, and the order form records them.
  7. The access we need. You give us access to outputs, exceptions and settings, and no more than we need.
  8. A signed order form. It names the workflows covered, the fee and the other details listed below. We start work when both of us have signed it.

What a retainer includes

Each retainer covers the following, to the extent the order form sets out.

  • Monitoring. Regular checks of the workflow’s outputs against the agreed measures, and a review of exceptions and failures. The order form says how often. Unless it says otherwise, that is monthly.
  • Changes. Adjustments to the workflow within the monthly change allowance stated in the order form.
  • Support. Answers to questions from your named users, during the support hours in the order form.
  • Reporting. A short report each month covering what we checked, what went wrong, what we changed and what we recommend. Every three months we also review whether the retainer is still justified.
  • A change log. A record of every change we make to the workflow, with the date and the reason.

What the order form sets out

  • the workflow or workflows covered, and the start date;
  • the monthly fee;
  • how often we monitor, the monthly change allowance and the support hours;
  • the measures and review points we agreed;
  • the information and tools the workflow may use; and
  • your named owner and cover, and our named contact.

Not included unless agreed in writing

  • New workflows, which start with their own process review.
  • Significant new capabilities or connections to other systems, which we scope and price separately.
  • Training sessions for new groups of staff, which we quote separately.
  • Work to fix problems caused by changes to your systems or processes that you did not tell us about.
  • Fees charged by the AI tools and software the workflow uses. You pay those directly to the provider, unless the order form says otherwise.

What we commit to

These are our obligations to you under a retainer.

  1. Reasonable skill and care. We provide the services with reasonable skill and care.
  2. What the order form says. We do the monitoring and reporting it sets out, at the frequency it sets out.
  3. Prompt replies. We acknowledge a support request within one working day and give you an answer or a plan within three working days.
  4. Telling you plainly. If we find that the workflow is producing wrong, unsafe or inappropriate outputs, we tell you as soon as we know, and in any case within one working day, and we recommend whether to pause it. We tell you when the cause is our own change or mistake.
  5. Review points stay. We do not remove or weaken a human review point without your written agreement.
  6. Changes are recorded and agreed. We record every change in the change log. We agree with you first before making a change that affects what leaves your firm, who reviews what, or which information the workflow uses.
  7. Approved tools and information only. We use only the tools and information listed in the order form, or that you approve in writing afterwards.
  8. Confidentiality and data protection. As set out in your information, below.
  9. A clean handover. When a retainer ends, we give you what you need to keep running the workflow yourself or with someone else (see reviewing and ending).

If we do not meet one of these commitments, tell us. We will put it right at no extra charge where we can. The section on responsibility and liability sets out what else applies.

What we need from you

For a retainer to work, you agree to:

  • keep the review points staffed and check outputs as agreed;
  • tell us in good time about changes that affect the workflow, such as new tools, new people in key roles, changes to your processes, and changes to the rules that apply to your work;
  • give us the access, information and answers we ask for within a reasonable time;
  • keep to the information rules in the order form, and make sure you are allowed to give us, and to use in the workflow, any information you provide, including personal data;
  • keep the log-ins and access we set up secure, and share them only with your named users; and
  • pay our invoices on time.

AI can produce outputs that are wrong or incomplete. That is why a person reviews what matters. Your firm remains responsible for what it sends out, for decisions made using the workflow, and for its own professional and regulatory obligations. We do not give legal, accounting, tax, medical or other regulated professional advice.

Fees and payment

The monthly fee is set in the order form. Our current range is £400 to £900 per month. Where your fee falls in that range depends on how many workflows are covered, how much work passes through them, how often we monitor, and how much change allowance you need.

  • Fees are in pounds sterling and exclude VAT, which we add where it applies.
  • We invoice monthly in advance. Invoices are payable within 14 days.
  • We will not change the monthly fee in the first six months. After that, we may change it on 60 days’ written notice. If you do not accept the change, you may end the retainer before it takes effect, and the old fee applies until then.
  • The monthly change allowance does not carry over to the next month unless the order form says it does.
  • If an invoice is overdue, we may send a written reminder. If it then stays unpaid, we may pause changes and routine support until it is paid. We will still tell you promptly about any serious problem we know of with the workflow.
  • Statutory interest and compensation for late payment apply under the Late Payment of Commercial Debts (Interest) Act 1998.

Reviewing and ending a retainer

  • Every three months we review with your named owner whether the retainer is still justified, using the agreed measures. If it is not, we recommend reducing or ending it, and you decide.
  • There is no minimum term. Either of us may end the retainer at any time by giving 30 days’ written notice.
  • Either of us may end it straight away by written notice if the other commits a serious breach and does not put it right within 14 days of being asked in writing, or becomes insolvent.
  • If we end the retainer for any reason other than your breach, or you end it because of our serious breach, we refund any fee you paid for a period after the end date.

When a retainer ends

  • Within 14 days, we give you documentation of how the workflow is set up, including its instructions, settings, review points and change log, so that you or someone else can keep running it.
  • We stop accessing your systems and information on the end date. Within 30 days we delete or return the information we hold for you, unless the law requires us to keep it. We keep our own business records, such as invoices, for as long as the law requires.

Your information

  • We keep your confidential information confidential. We use it only to provide the services, and share it only with people and providers who need it for that and are bound to keep it confidential. This does not cover information that is public through no fault of ours, or that the law requires us to disclose.
  • We do not put your information into any tool or service you have not approved.
  • Please do not give us confidential client information or sensitive personal data unless the workflow needs it and the order form says so.
  • Where we handle personal data on your behalf, you are the controller and we are your processor. We will handle it only on your written instructions and under a written data processing agreement, which we agree with you before we start. It covers, among other things, the tools and providers we use, security, and what we do if there is a personal data breach. We tell you without undue delay if we become aware of a breach affecting your data.

How we handle the details you send us in an enquiry, before we work together, is in our privacy notice.

Responsibility and liability

Nothing in these terms limits liability that cannot be limited by law, including liability for death or personal injury caused by negligence, or for fraud. Subject to that:

  • our total liability to you in connection with a retainer, whether for breach of contract, negligence or otherwise, is limited to the fees you paid us for that retainer in the 12 months before the event that gave rise to the claim;
  • we are not liable for loss of profit, revenue, goodwill or anticipated savings, or for indirect or consequential loss;
  • we are not responsible for failures caused by tools, models or services we do not control, such as an outage or a change by a provider, as long as we told you promptly once we knew and did what we reasonably could to limit the effect; and
  • we are not responsible for loss that results from outputs being used without the review the order form requires.

Ownership

  • You own your information and what the workflow produces for your business.
  • What we configure specifically for your workflow, such as its instructions, settings and checklists, is yours to keep using after the retainer ends.
  • We keep our general methods, templates and know-how, and may use them for other clients. We do not use your confidential information when we do.
  • Third-party tools stay subject to their providers’ own terms.

General terms

  • Businesses only. These terms are for businesses and professional practices acting for business purposes. We do not offer retainers to consumers.
  • The whole agreement. The order form, these terms and any data processing agreement are the whole agreement about the retainer. If they conflict, the data processing agreement prevails on personal data, then the order form, then these terms.
  • Changes to these terms. The version on this page on the day you sign the order form applies to your retainer. If we change the terms later, we give you 30 days’ written notice before the new version applies to you. If you do not accept it, you may end the retainer before it takes effect.
  • Events outside our control. Neither of us is in breach for a delay or failure caused by something outside our reasonable control. This does not excuse late payment.
  • Transfer and subcontractors. Neither of us may transfer the retainer to someone else without the other’s written agreement. We may use subcontractors we have told you about, and we remain responsible for their work.
  • Notices. Notices must be in writing and sent to the contact named in the order form, or to ZASTRAS LTD at its registered office. Email counts as writing.
  • If you are unhappy. Tell us in writing, using the contact named in the order form. We will acknowledge your complaint promptly and reply in writing with what we propose to do.
  • Governing law. These terms, and any dispute connected with them, are governed by Scots law, and the courts of Scotland have exclusive jurisdiction.
