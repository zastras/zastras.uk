Who we are

Zastras Practical AI is a service of ZASTRAS LTD, a company registered in Scotland under number SC832156. Our registered office is 48 West George Street, 2nd Floor, Glasgow, Scotland, G2 1BP.

This notice explains how we handle personal information when you visit our website or send us an initial enquiry through its contact form. It does not cover work for clients, which is described separately.

Information you give us

When you contact us, we collect the name, email address and message you provide. You may also choose to give us your organisation’s name. Please do not include confidential client information or sensitive personal data in an initial enquiry.

We need your contact details and message to respond. If you do not provide them, we may be unable to answer your enquiry.

As with any website or form, the services named below necessarily receive technical information such as your IP address when your browser requests a page or sends the form.

Why we use it

We use enquiry information to respond to you, understand what help you are seeking and decide whether our services may be relevant. Our lawful basis is our legitimate interests under Article 6(1)(f) of the UK GDPR: handling enquiries sent to our business and considering potential work with the person or organisation contacting us. We limit the information requested and do not use contact-form submissions to enrol people in a mailing list.

Who can access it

Only Lex Sobrinho and ZASTRAS LTD team members specifically authorised to handle enquiries may access them. We use Formspree to receive contact-form submissions and our email provider to deliver and manage the resulting messages. These providers process information to provide their services to us. We do not sell enquiry information.

How long we keep it

If an enquiry does not lead to a client relationship, we keep the correspondence for up to 12 months after our last exchange, then delete it, unless we need it for longer to establish, exercise or defend a legal claim. If you become a client, we will provide information about how we handle records relating to that work.

Processing outside the UK

Formspree processes contact-form submissions in the United States. The safeguard we rely on for this transfer is the UK Extension to the EU–US Data Privacy Framework (the UK–US data bridge), under which Formspree is certified. You can ask us for further details about that safeguard, including how to obtain a copy, using the contact details below.

Website hosting

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages, provided by GitHub, Inc. in the United States. GitHub states that when a GitHub Pages site is visited, the visitor’s IP address is logged and stored for security purposes. See the GitHub privacy statement.

Cookies and tracking

We do not use analytics or advertising cookies to track visitors to this website. This website does not load scripts, fonts or other resources from third parties.

If you send the contact form with JavaScript switched off, your browser is taken to a confirmation page run by Formspree, which sets a cookie and loads fonts from Google. Formspree’s privacy policy applies to that page.

Your rights

Depending on the circumstances, you may ask us to give you access to your personal information, correct it, delete it or restrict how we use it.

You also have the right to object to our use of your information where we rely on legitimate interests.

To make a request or raise a concern, use the website contact form or write to ZASTRAS LTD at the registered office address above. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint.
