Who we are

Zastras Practical AI is a service of ZASTRAS LTD, a company registered in Scotland (company number SC832156). Our registered office is 48 West George Street, 2nd Floor, Glasgow, Scotland, G2 1BP.

ZASTRAS LTD receives your enquiry and decides how the details in it are used. Under UK data protection law that makes us the “controller” of that information.

What we collect and why

The contact form asks for the following. We use the details to reply to you, to understand whether we can help, and to keep a sensible record of the conversation.

Name
So we know who we are talking to and can address you properly.
Work email
So we can reply. The form service also uses it as the reply-to address on the notification we receive.
Organisation (optional)
To understand the kind of business you run.
What would you like to improve?
To understand the process you describe and judge whether we can help.

The form also includes a hidden field that people never see or fill in. If it is filled in, the form service treats the submission as automated spam and discards it.

The form service may also record technical details when you submit the form, such as your IP address and browser information. Its own privacy policy describes this. We do not use these details to profile you.

Please do not include confidential client information or sensitive personal data in your enquiry. We do not need it at this stage.

We do not use enquiries to build a mailing list, and we do not sell your details.

Choose and confirm it (for many business enquiries this is “legitimate interests”, but that is for the owner and any adviser to decide). (2) Check what technical details the Formspree dashboard and notification email actually show, and edit the sentence about technical details to match.

Who handles your details

Formspree: the contact form service

The form is provided by Formspree, Inc. When you press “Send enquiry”, your browser sends the details directly to Formspree. Formspree stores the submission for us and sends us an email notification. It acts on our behalf for this purpose. Formspree’s privacy policy is at formspree.io/legal/privacy-policy.

If you send the form with JavaScript switched off, your browser is taken to a page on Formspree’s own website, where Formspree’s terms and cookies apply.

Cookies and tracking

This website does not use analytics, advertising or tracking tools, and it does not set cookies of its own. Its fonts are hosted on the site itself, so your browser does not contact a font provider.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have the right to:

  • ask for a copy of the personal information we hold about you;
  • ask us to correct information that is wrong or incomplete;
  • ask us to delete your information, in some circumstances;
  • ask us to restrict how we use your information, in some circumstances;
  • object to us using your information, in some circumstances; and
  • ask for the information you gave us in a portable format, where that right applies.

We will respond within one month of receiving a request. These rights are not absolute, and we will explain if one does not apply.

Contacting us and complaints

To ask about your information or use any of your rights, use the contact form and say that your message is about your personal information, or write to us at the registered office address above.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint. We would welcome the chance to put things right first, but you do not have to contact us before complaining.
