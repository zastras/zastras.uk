Who we are

Zastras Practical AI is a service of ZASTRAS LTD, a company registered in Scotland under number SC832156. Our registered office is 48 West George Street, 2nd Floor, Glasgow, Scotland, G2 1BP.

This notice explains how we handle personal information when you visit our website or send us an initial enquiry through its contact form. It does not cover work for clients, which is described separately.

Information you give us

When you contact us, we collect the name, email address and message you provide. You may also choose to give us your organisation’s name. Please do not include confidential client information or sensitive personal data in an initial enquiry.

We need your contact details and message to respond. If you do not provide them, we may be unable to answer your enquiry.

As with any website or form, the services named below necessarily receive technical information such as your IP address when your browser requests a page or sends the form.

Why we use it

We use enquiry information to respond to you, understand what help you are seeking and decide whether our services may be relevant. Our lawful basis is our legitimate interests under Article 6(1)(f) of the UK GDPR: handling enquiries sent to our business and considering potential work with the person or organisation contacting us. We limit the information requested and do not use contact-form submissions to enrol people in a mailing list.

Who can access it

Only Lex Sobrinho and ZASTRAS LTD team members specifically authorised to handle enquiries may access them. We use Formspree to receive contact-form submissions and our email provider to deliver and manage the resulting messages. These providers process information to provide their services to us. We do not sell enquiry information.

Verify before publishing: access This could not be checked from outside. Confirm who can log in to the Formspree account (the Free plan includes one login) and who can read the receiving mailbox, and that no one else can: assistants, shared inboxes, forwarding rules, or the Formspree notification list (up to two addresses on the Free plan). Formspree’s own security page says its staff access to customer data is limited to authorised personnel. Also confirm the name of the email provider for internal records; the notice does not name it.

How long we keep it

[Retention period and deletion process to be confirmed before publication.]

Not published: retention (owner decision) The proposed 12-month wording is held back because the deletion process cannot yet be enforced. Findings from Formspree’s plans and account-limits pages: every submission is stored until it is deleted; on the Free plan the dashboard shows only the last 30 days, and older submissions are not deleted but become invisible unless you upgrade. No automatic expiry and no switch to turn storage off was found in Formspree’s documentation, and submissions filed as spam are stored too. So the Formspree copy can meet a 12-month limit only if (a) submissions are deleted by hand from the dashboard inside the 30-day window, as a monthly routine, with the mailbox as the record, or (b) the account moves to a paid plan with full history and submissions are deleted at 12 months. The mailbox copy needs its own routine, for example a dedicated label reviewed quarterly. Once one of these is adopted and working, publish: “If an enquiry does not lead to a client relationship, we keep the correspondence for up to 12 months after our last exchange, then delete it, unless we need it for longer to establish, exercise or defend a legal claim. If you become a client, we will provide information about how we handle records relating to that work.” A step-by-step routine is in the project’s internal records.

Processing outside the UK

Formspree processes contact-form submissions in the United States. The safeguard we rely on for this transfer is [UK transfer safeguard to be confirmed]. You can ask us for further details about that safeguard, including how to obtain a copy, using the contact details below.

Not published: UK transfer safeguard (owner decision) Verified: Formspree’s security page says its services are hosted with Amazon Web Services in the United States and that it relies on Standard Contractual Clauses “as a data processor”; its privacy policy confirms processing in the United States. Not found: any published data processing agreement, any UK Addendum or International Data Transfer Agreement, or any statement about UK transfers (formspree.io/legal lists only the Terms of Service, Privacy Policy and Security page). Reference to EU Standard Contractual Clauses alone does not establish the UK mechanism. A Data Privacy Framework listing for Formspree exists, but its current status could not be checked from this build, and a third-party report says it is inactive, so it cannot be relied on yet. Next step: ask Formspree for its signed data processing terms including the UK Addendum (or an IDTA) and its transfer-risk information, keep a copy, then name the safeguard here. Formspree’s privacy policy also describes API users as independent controllers while its security page says it acts as a processor, so ask it to confirm its role in the same request.

Website hosting

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages, provided by GitHub, Inc. in the United States. GitHub states that when a GitHub Pages site is visited, the visitor’s IP address is logged and stored for security purposes. See the GitHub privacy statement.

Addition and open check: hosting This section was not in the supplied wording. It is included because the audit confirmed GitHub receives every visitor’s IP address and GitHub’s own documentation says it logs it. Keep it unless you decide otherwise. The UK safeguard for GitHub’s US processing has not been verified; if you want the notice to name one, check GitHub’s current terms.

Cookies and tracking

We do not use analytics or advertising cookies to track visitors to this website. This website does not load scripts, fonts or other resources from third parties.

If you send the contact form with JavaScript switched off, your browser is taken to a confirmation page run by Formspree, which sets a cookie and loads fonts from Google. Formspree’s privacy policy applies to that page.

Audit result (live preview, 23 November 2026) Home and privacy pages: no cookies, no local or session storage, no service workers, and every request goes to this site (fonts are self-hosted). Form with JavaScript: one request to formspree.io, whose response carries a cookie header named fs_ab1; Chrome did not store it because it is a cross-site response. Form without JavaScript: the visitor lands on formspree.io/thanks, which sets fs_ab1 and loads Inter and Source Code Pro from Google Fonts, so Google receives the visitor’s IP address. Formspree does not document what fs_ab1 is for (the value is “control”, which suggests an A/B test), so no purpose is stated above. No anti-spam challenge appeared, because reCAPTCHA is switched off. No cookie banner is needed for this build: nothing this site runs sets cookies or storage; the hand-off page belongs to Formspree. If reCAPTCHA is switched back on, repeat the audit and add the provider and purpose here. To keep no-JavaScript visitors on this site, add a thank-you page and a _next redirect once the domain is live.

Your rights

Depending on the circumstances, you may ask us to give you access to your personal information, correct it, delete it or restrict how we use it.

You also have the right to object to our use of your information where we rely on legitimate interests.

To make a request or raise a concern, use the website contact form or write to ZASTRAS LTD at the registered office address above. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint.
